# Frozen prototype banks in causal graphs

`PrototypeRootBase` wraps exactly an authored `FrozenPrototypeRoot` or independently sampled `SampledPrototypeRoot`. Its `prototype-root-base-v1` archive is accepted by functional node v2 and standalone network roots. Batch-reference records remain rejected: their bank count and emission population depend on the declared batch and are a distinct lifecycle.

Every emission draws fresh row weights and multiplies each weight vector by the entire frozen bank. The common root helper carries this behavior through functional prefixes, numerical graphs, native scalar/vector graphs, typed converter graphs, standalone networks, and support/query population splitting. Prefix raw ledgers retain integer multinomial counts or Gamma values. Keyed task generation and original-row inspection reconstruct the same rows without refitting the bank. Complete sampled construction plans, raw records and RNG states remain nested in archived recipes.

Certificates are conditional on the realized bank: the finite convex hull is bounded and all nonconstant coordinates share a weight source. The construction law's moment order remains separately recorded; a bank sampled from a heavy-tailed law does not become an unconditional finite-variance population. Independent target calibration and network variance matching can fit this conditional frozen population. No prototype-output normalization is silently fitted from task/query rows.

A prototype construction base can itself retain `SampledSourceRootBase` selection and independent normalization custody. Decoder caller limits propagate through that nested record. Graph admission accounts for fresh row-weight/value buffers, retained prototype data, bank reconstruction and any nested source calibration replay, aggregated with the rest of the graph before execution. Failed or malformed records fail closed.

Public tests cover literal `weights @ prototypes`, retained count values, authored/sampled banks, native continuous/binary mixtures, typed converter readouts, network matching, conditional calibration, strict replay/tamper, saved pair reopening and repeated original-row traces. Evidence is ignored under `docs/evidence/issue-28-prototype-root-graphs/`. This library adapter does not provide an explorer constructor or a paper-exact beta/count convention; those distinctions remain in [the core contract](issue-28-prototype-roots.md). Source profile sampling of prototype parameters, output-normalization profiles and standalone prototype replacement controls remain required follow-ons.
