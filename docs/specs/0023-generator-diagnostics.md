# Issue 23 generator coverage and family diagnostics

Status: Q1–Q39 accepted. Final shared-understanding confirmation pending. The user explicitly prioritizes generator variety across TabPFN, MITRA, LimiX, TabICL and additional supported sources. Implementation has not been authorized or started.

Issue: https://github.com/Avistian/r0/issues/23

## Accepted decisions: round 1

1. Deliver both a source-backed research catalog and a working diagnostic pipeline over bounded tiny representative tasks. Documents alone cannot demonstrate duplicate detection, label integrity or filtering bias.
2. Catalog broad families (linear/sparse, smooth/GP, tree/piecewise, SCM/neural, interactions, mixtures and observation processes), but implement only the representatives needed to demonstrate diagnostics. Distinguish proposed, supported, measured and deferred entries. Production families remain #27–29 and observation/mixture machinery #30. Catalog presence is not implementation evidence.
3. Audit pinned versions of TabPFN, TabICL, TabForestPFN and MITRA; add LimiX explicitly at the user's request and extend the shortlist when primary sources warrant it. Distinguish paper descriptions, source-revision evidence, independent reconstructions and local measurements. Undisclosed behavior is unknown, not a demonstrated weakness. Preserve the repository no-copy rule.
4. Separate correctness, mechanism coverage, task diversity, difficulty, observation conditions, rejection bias and generation cost. Do not invent one composite quality score. Downstream usefulness is unmeasured until controlled learner experiments establish it.
5. Label checks follow the declared generative process. Independently recompute deterministic targets; distinguish corrupted targets from legitimate noisy realizations. Preserve enough diagnostic information to explain target construction. A noisy label differing from its noiseless function is not by itself a failure.
6. Cover mechanisms crossed with feature types, relevant/nuisance features, sparsity, interaction order, noise, imbalance, missingness, subpopulations, context/query shifts and table shape. Distinguish supported, unsupported and unexplored combinations. Do not assume every Cartesian combination is meaningful or every empty cell defective. Catalog relational extensions, but scope executable work to the single-table milestone.
7. Separate hard invalidity from experimental selection among valid tasks. Invalid schema/inconsistent provenance are correctness failures. High noise, weak signal, unusual distributions or imbalance are measured properties, not automatic rejections. Optional selection filters retain their rule, reasons and before/after distributions.
8. Demonstrate response to duplicate tasks, incorrect labels, lost coverage and biased filtering. Include legitimate noisy tasks and harmless transformed equivalents as controls. Retain readable task cards and trace a sparse/high-noise cell from proposals through rejection to accepted share.
9. Produce a ranked falsifiable experiment backlog, not a selected production recipe. Each hypothesis records the suspected gap, evidence, intervention, expected benefit, possible regressions, cost and promotion/rejection criteria. Retain complementary combinations and alternative explanations. Broad superiority requires the controlled experiments in #36.

## Accepted decisions: round 2

10. Distinguish materially different prior recipes within model families. Cover original reproducible recipes and later versions that change generation mechanisms. Record paper, repository-default, training-configuration and released-checkpoint evidence separately. Do not spend audit effort on checkpoint variants with no generator information.
11. Use a compositional catalog: graph/dependency construction; node, edge or standalone function families; feature/noise distributions; target construction; observation transformations; hyperpriors, mixtures and selection policies. Distinguish standalone MLP generators from MLP functions inside SCMs.
12. Demonstrate diagnostics across six bounded representative families: sparse linear, smooth/GP, standalone random MLP, small SCM, tree/piecewise and explicit interaction rules. Declare limitations. Production flexibility/optimization remain #27–29. At least one representative must trace to a documented published recipe component, without claiming reproduction of an entire released model's training distribution.
13. Give every proposal an identity and recorded outcome, including failures. Retain requested family/regime, seed, sampled parameters, generation status, transformations, validity findings, selection decision and rejection reasons. Generation failure is distinct from valid-but-rejected output. Explain how requested coverage became realized coverage.
14. Distinguish identical observed task content, repeated sampled mechanisms with different observations, and equivalence under explicitly supported transformations such as row order or consistent class renaming. Count separately. Do not claim universal semantic equivalence. Repeated mechanisms may be intentional; diagnose concentration instead of automatically rejecting them.
15. Each representative exposes an independently checkable target-construction record, including noiseless values, realized noise or label-randomization events, and final targets where applicable. Acceptance uses hand-derived cases or independently written checks, not the generator's target function called twice. Diagnostic records remain outside predictor inputs.
16. Report per-cell absolute counts and shares for proposals, successful generations, valid tasks, selected tasks and rejection reasons. State denominators. Distinguish zero observed, unsupported and not measured. Declare bins before inspecting results. Small samples establish accounting, not reliable probability estimates.
17. Separate structural difficulty (relevant features, interaction order, noise settings, subgroup rarity) from predictive difficulty measured by a probe. A probe records its learner, split, preprocessing and budget. Default acceptance uses inspectable mechanisms and known answers. Optional fixed CPU probes may be specified later, but cannot silently become quality rankings or filtering rules.
18. Include an explicitly biased demonstration selection filter, such as rejecting high-noise tasks, and an unfiltered reference. Success means diagnosing the resulting coverage loss, not producing cleaner-looking accepted tables. Production/adaptive selection belongs to #30.
19. Give every cataloged component an explicit implementation disposition: existing ticket, proposed follow-up or justified deferral. Preserve source-backed options beyond #23's six representatives so the catalog becomes an actionable implementation backlog.

## Accepted decisions: round 3

20. Initial representative mechanisms: sparse linear coefficients with relevant/nuisance features; joint smooth/GP function draws at sampled coordinates under a declared kernel; standalone random MLP weights mapping inputs to latent targets; small SCM DAGs with recorded equations, observed variables and hidden causes; sampled tree splits/leaf values; explicit product, parity/XOR and gated interaction rules. Separate mechanism instances from realized observations. Elaborate variants remain cataloged and assigned to later tickets.
21. Support regression and declared binary/three-class target adaptations where mathematically appropriate. Thresholds or class rules are sampled independently of realized query labels, followed by a task-wide class-label mapping. Retain latent targets, thresholds/rules, mappings and any label-noise events. Do not silently force balance or resample until every class appears. Declare the complete vocabulary even when classes are absent from observations.
22. Use distinct reproducible streams for mechanism sampling, observations, episode splitting and diagnostic interventions. Derive streams from explicit root seeds and stable task keys; request order must not change a task. Support fixed mechanism/new observations and fixed source task/new partition. Retain stream identities and resolved parameters.
23. Priors owns representative samplers; a diagnostics module inspects generation records and computes coverage/findings; replay owns configuration, execution and reporting. Diagnostics may inspect hidden mechanisms; learner-facing inputs retain the existing restricted predictor view. Diagnostics never silently regenerate or replace an undesirable task.
24. Content identity covers ordered schema, observed values, missingness and targets, excluding incidental IDs. Mechanism identity covers family/version and realized rule parameters, excluding observation draws. Initially supported equivalences are row permutation and consistent bijective class renaming; retain row multiplicity. Track episode partitions separately. Feature permutations, numeric rescaling and broader functional equivalence remain explicit future extensions.
25. Class membership, mappings, masks and discrete rules require exact agreement. Float64 constructions use atol=1e-10 and rtol=1e-10 initially, retaining expected/observed values and differences. GP verification inspects joint sampling construction and realized noise, never treating a function sample as a posterior mean. Tolerance exceptions require justification before acceptance.
26. Hard failures are incorrect labels under the declared construction, invalid records, broken accounting, failed replay and violated explicit coverage expectations. Concentration, imbalance, weak signal and selection-induced coverage changes are reported, not automatic invalidity. Acceptance scenarios declare expected findings so a deliberately biased filter demonstrates successful detection.
27. Default demonstration: 72 source-task proposals = six representatives × two noise regimes × three root seeds × two mechanism draws. Each has 64 rows split into 48 context and 16 query rows, with at most eight features where supported. Per-invocation caps: 256 proposals, 128 rows/task, 16 features, three classes. Serial CPU generation; retain failures and never silently top up rejections. Record elapsed time. No learner fitting in default acceptance. Limits apply to diagnostic runs, not production generators.
28. Audit completeness requires versioned records for TabPFN, MITRA, LimiX, TabICL and TabForestPFN. Map every substantive generator option in the HTML guide to a catalog component, existing-component duplicate, unsupported claim awaiting evidence, or justified exclusion. Unknown hyperparameters/unavailable recipes remain explicit. Completeness means accounting for evidence and gaps, not recovering undisclosed recipes.
29. Rank investigation by evidence for the suspected gap, task relevance, complementarity and implementation/measurement cost. Retain unselected candidates and rationale. Promotion to implementation/ablation requires a reproducible mechanism description, diagnostic acceptance criteria, explicit comparison and bounded budget. Production-mixture promotion requires later downstream evidence.

## Accepted decisions: round 4

30. The two demonstration regimes mean no added observation noise versus explicitly specified added observation noise. Regression uses additive Gaussian target noise with standard deviation 0 or 1 in declared target units. Classification uses uniform label replacement with probability 0 or 0.2 over the declared vocabulary, recording whether the label actually changed. Internal SCM randomness remains separate. These settings do not imply equal signal-to-noise ratios across families.
31. Each family/noise cell has six proposals across three seeds and two draws. Assign two proposals each to regression, binary and three-class tasks with a declared rotating schedule. Pair noise regimes using the same underlying mechanism, features, target adapter and episode partition; change only added observation noise. Missing observed classes remain valid.
32. Declare small demonstration ranges: sparse linear has 1–4 relevant features among at most 8 and signed coefficients; smooth/GP uses one-dimensional RBF draws with explicit signal variance and length scale; random MLP has 1–2 hidden layers, widths 4–8 and tanh/ReLU; small SCM has at most 6 nodes and chain/fork/collider examples with explicit hidden variables; tree/piecewise has depth 1–3 with explicit thresholds and leaf values; interactions use degree 2–3 products, parity/XOR and gated rules. Every distribution, range and resolved parameter belongs in versioned configuration. Broader architectures, kernels, graphs and hyperpriors remain eligible later.
33. Expose r0 diagnose-priors --config … --out … with strict versioned configuration. Produce an immutable run directory containing resolved configuration, proposal records, task cards, coverage tables, findings and a readable walkthrough summary. Separate raw/hidden diagnostic records from predictor inputs. Reuse an existing directory only when contents are identical.
34. The default acceptance manifest specifies the 72 proposals and their exact family/noise/task-type allocations, complete outcome accounting and no unexplained generation failures. It does not impose scientific-quality score cutoffs. Zero-denominator shares are null with a reason. Empty accepted cells retain the distinction between generation failure, invalidity and selection.
35. Catalog entries have separate fields for source support/unresolved claims, proposed/implemented/deferred capability, locally measured coverage, and downstream usefulness (initially NOT_MEASURED). Paper claims, implementation and usefulness are distinct evidence states.
36. Preserve failed proposal records and completed reports before returning nonzero for diagnostic failures. Unexpected exceptions are not ordinary selection rejections. In injected-defect walkthroughs, distinguish a diagnostic failure from acceptance passing because that failure was expected.
37. Acceptance compares unfiltered versus biased selection on the same proposals; exact duplicates versus repeated mechanisms/new observations; row permutation and class-renaming equivalents; a corrupted target with generating rule/noise unchanged; a missing proposal or coverage allocation; legitimate noisy tasks; request-order replay; and query-target isolation. Show affected records, counts, findings and expected outcomes, not only a final status.
38. Retain independent worked calculations for at least one task from every representative: a linear row, tiny neural forward pass, SCM ancestry, tree path, interaction rule and small GP sampling construction. Include realized noise and target adaptation. Walk one source-backed published component and one proposed r0 extension from citation to configuration, task card and measured coverage; label the extension as a hypothesis.
39. Produce local run identities and artifact paths usable by later tracking integration. Do not introduce live W&B integration or learner training. Deliver the source audit, six representatives, diagnostics, retained walkthrough and mapped experiment backlog. Production generators, adaptive selection, independent held-out challenges and controlled downstream comparisons remain assigned to later tickets.

## Consolidated delivery scope

- A versioned compositional generator catalog and primary-source audit covering the named peers and substantive options in the supplied HTML guide. Explicitly retain unknowns, reproduction gaps and later-ticket dispositions.
- Six bounded executable representative families with replayable mechanisms, observations, noise and target adaptations; broad catalog coverage does not imply those options are already implemented.
- Public priors/diagnostics/replay boundaries and a strict diagnostic CLI producing immutable, inspectable artifacts.
- The 72-proposal paired-noise demonstration, separate content/mechanism/equivalence counts, complete filtering accounting and declared coverage expectations.
- Independent worked examples, deliberate-defect controls, retained failure evidence, and a ranked falsifiable experiment backlog.

No production prior mixture, architecture, benchmark protocol or superiority claim is selected. No new ADR is needed for these reversible diagnostic implementation choices; this specification is their authoritative record.
